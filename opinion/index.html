Opinion

Argued, not reported.

Everything else on this site tries to stay on the record and off the soapbox. The pieces here do the opposite: each takes a position and argues it, under my name and with my affiliations stated. The facts in them are held to the same standard as the reports, and each piece sources them at the end.

CT 55th House District · October 2026

The Votes Are the Job

Why it is fair, and necessary, for Ellen Paul's campaign to make this race about Rep. Steve Weir's record. An incumbent starts with the title on the ballot, a caucus web page, and state-paid newsletters; what he carries that a challenger does not is two terms of recorded votes. Asking voters to read them is not a smear, it is the job review. Written by the co-chair of the Andover Democratic Town Committee, who says so up front, with every figure sourced to the official roll calls.
