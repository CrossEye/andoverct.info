OCR Review: 24_ordinance.md
===========================

A proofreading pass over `24_ordinance.md`, the OCR text recovered from the
Town of Andover ordinance compilation (`24_ordinance.pdf`, 97 pages). The goal
is to separate OCR artifacts, which should be fixed, from typos that are in the
Town's original, which should be preserved.

Page numbers below are the printed page numbers from the `<!-- p. N -->`
markers. Please verify each item against the scan before changing it; the
"verify" sections especially.

Suggested handling:

  - **Section 1:** fix these unless the scan shows otherwise.
  - **Section 2:** check each against the scan; fix only where the scan
    disagrees with the text.
  - **Section 3:** check the scan for dropped or garbled lines.
  - **Section 4:** leave as-is (original typos), since the header says spelling
    follows the scan.

### Apostrophes ###

Possessive apostrophes are missing almost everywhere (`Towns Commission`,
`members years of active service`, `Officers satisfaction`), while a few
survive (`Employee's`, `day's`). That pattern looks more like systematic OCR
loss than the original. A spot check of a few pages of the scan would settle
whether to restore them globally.



3. Possible dropped or garbled lines
------------------------------------

These are the highest-value checks, since a whole line may be missing.

  - **p. 9, Garden Apartment Code § 4.3.** The sentence says the Inspector may
    "make inspections ... provided, that such rules and regulations shall not
    be in conflict," referring to rules it never mentions. The Rental Code's
    parallel § 4.3 (p. 12) reads "make and to adopt such written rules and
    regulations as may be necessary." A line was likely skipped, either in the
    OCR or in the original typing.
  - **p. 12, Rental Code § 3.** "survey at all reasonable times all dwellings,
    dwelling units or portion thereof, or person in charge" is missing the
    "The owner and/or occupant of every..." clause that appears in the Garden
    Apartment version (p. 8). It then repeats "at all reasonable hours for the
    purpose" twice.
  - **p. 53, definition 3.** "Emergency Medical Technician-Basic (EMT-8) level
    by the Connecticut Office..." is a fragment. It probably should begin
    "Emergency Medical Technician certification is defined as certification
    to the..."
  - **p. 64, Ethics § 7.** Rules run A through H, then "Financial Benefit"
    with no letter, then J. The `I.` label was lost, probably read as a stray
    mark.
  - **p. 68, Ethics § 8.** Same pattern: H is followed by an unlabeled "No
    complaint may be made..." before J. Another lost `I.`
  - **p. 74.** The fracking definitions run `f.` through `k.` where items 5
    and following should be. This may be OCR list detection rather than the
    text itself.



4. Likely typos in the original (leave as-is)
---------------------------------------------

These look like the Town's own errors: typist misspellings rather than
character-shape confusions. Preserve them unless producing a corrected edition.

  - Misspellings: `CONNECTIUT`, `ASEEMBLY`, `OFFICALS`, `REQURIEMENTS`,
    `ENFORECEMENT`, `PRESCIBING`, `ENERYGY`, `CONSTURCTION`, `RECYCABLE`,
    `BUISNESS`, `PREMIT`, `ABTEMENT`, `PROVIDIING`, `AMMENDMENT`,
    `ORIDINANCE`, `ASSESTMENT` (index)
  - Wording errors: `eight (80)`, `nay act`, `shall obtain shall obtain`,
    `in a matter in a matter`, `the total of the total`
  - p. 34: "GLASS FOOD CONTAINER means a high-density polyethylene bottle"
    (copy-paste from the HDPE definition)
  - p. 26: `Section 11-8` refers to a section that doesn't exist here
    (probably carried over from a model ordinance)
  - p. 46: `Section 15(c) of this Ordinance` (the ordinance has no § 15)


### Numbering and index ###

Also probably source problems rather than OCR, but worth noting:

  - Ordinance numbers are inconsistent: `01-16` for a 2016 ordinance, `01-06`
    for a 2006 one, and `21-05` sitting between `04-21` and `24-01` (likely
    meant `05-21`).
  - Some index page references don't match the printed pages. For example,
    OFFICE HOURS is indexed at p. 26 but printed on p. 29, and DRIVEWAYS
    (p. 3) points to ordinance 60-03, which has been repealed and does not
    appear there.
