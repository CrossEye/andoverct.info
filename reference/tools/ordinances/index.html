Transcription · Town ordinances · Passed April 23, 2024

Town of Andover Ordinances — compilation passed by Special Town Meeting, April 23, 2024

The town publishes its ordinances as a 97-page PDF that is, page for page, a photograph of a printout: no text layer, nothing to search, nothing to copy. Want to know whether there is an ordinance about campaign signs? You read all 97 pages, or you don't find out.

So I ran it through optical character recognition and rebuilt it as a document. The result keeps the compilation's own structure — the subject index, each ordinance under its title and number, the section headings, the nested clauses — and marks where each printed page begins, so the index's page references still work. The town's original wording is preserved exactly, including its own typos (it really does say CONNECTIUT on page 1). Where the OCR misread a character, and only there, the text was corrected against the scan; the two review notes below record every such decision.

⚠ Read this first

This is a machine transcription of a scan, checked in places but not proofread word by word. Single-character misreads may remain, and the layout of nested clauses follows the original typing, which is not always consistent. Bold and underlining inside paragraphs are not reproduced.

It is offered as a convenience for searching and reading. The scanned PDF from the town is the authority; for anything that matters, read the page there.
