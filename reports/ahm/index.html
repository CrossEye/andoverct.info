A Question for Five Candidates

One question, in the same words, to five Republican candidates on Connecticut ballots this November: will you publicly disavow Donald Trump? This page records who was asked, at what address, and on what date, and publishes every letter and reply in full and unedited, whatever it says. It also reads what the candidates have already said for themselves, namely every Meta political ad their own pages ran, read as text, as on-screen words, and as speech, plus two long-form interviews. The record is updated as answers come in, through Election Day.

Spending, Students, and Per-Pupil Costs

Ten years of federal school-finance data (NCES/Census F-33) for Andover, Hebron, Marlborough, and RHAM, with maps and charts for every group examined. Combined spending for the four districts grew 17% over the decade while inflation rose 29.5% — yet per-pupil cost climbed 68%, because enrollment fell 30%. The report widens the lens step by step, from the core towns through three contiguous rings of neighbors to 45 elementary-only peer districts and the state as a whole, showing the same denominator arithmetic at every scale.
