Subject: A question from a voter, and an offer to publish your answer in full

Dear Ms. Jennings,

I live in Andover, in the 4th Senate District, so you are on my ballot in November. I write about local government at andoverct.info, and I should say plainly that I co-chair the Andover Democratic Town Committee. This letter is my own, sent in my own name.

I have one question, and I would rather put it to you directly than guess at the answer.

Will you publicly disavow Donald Trump?

By that I mean a statement in your own name that he does not represent you and does not have your support. Not a comment on a single policy, and not a statement about the party in general.

I am asking the same question, in the same words, of Ryan Fazio, Steve Weir, and Jason Guidone. Whatever you send me I will publish in full and unedited, with the question and the date, at http://andoverct.info/reports/ahm/disavowal/. If you would rather answer somewhere else, in the Rivereast or on your own page, that is fine. Point me to it and I will link it.

If you have already answered this publicly and I have missed it, send me the link and I will say so.

I plan to publish on September 17, 2026 and to update through the election. There is no deadline. An answer at any point gets added.

Thank you for your time.
