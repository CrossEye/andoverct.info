Dear Ms. Jennings,

Thank you for your reply. You were the first of the five candidates to
answer, and you answered without hedging. You will not be surprised to hear
that I disagree with your answer, but I found your candor refreshing, and I
appreciate it very much.

I do want to offer a brief defense of the question. I did not ask it
because a state senator has any say over what a President does. I asked it
because Mr. Trump leads the party whose nomination you carry, and because
he is the most divisive political figure in my memory. Where a candidate
stands on him seems to me a fair thing for a voter to know, alongside her
views on taxes, schools, and housing, and not in place of them.

You invited another question, and I may well take you up on that. I should
be honest, though, that for me a refusal to disavow him is very likely
disqualifying, so I may not.

Your reply is now published in full at
http://andoverct.info/reports/ahm/disavowal/, and this note will be added
beside it.

Thank you again for your candor, and for taking the time to write such a
thoughtful response.
