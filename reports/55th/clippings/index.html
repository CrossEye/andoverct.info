Letters to the Editor: The 2026 Race for the 55th

Every letter to the editor the Rivereast News Bulletin printed about the 2026 contest for Connecticut’s 55th House District, between Democrat Ellen Paul and Republican State Rep. Steve Weir, from April 10, 2026 through October 30, 2026: 179 letters in 24 weekly editions, 102 on the Paul side and 77 on the Weir side. A letter’s side is whom it helps: Paul means it supports Ellen Paul or criticizes Steve Weir, and Weir the reverse. Each letter appears as the paper printed it and links to the original page in the paper’s online archive. The tags say what a letter argues, not whether it is right; filter by them, by the issues a letter touches, by the bills it names, or by writer.

About the compiler. I am Scott Sauyet. I co-chair the Andover Democratic Town Committee, I support Ellen Paul, and I wrote 13 of these letters. The library includes every letter on either side, reproduced as printed, without correction or reply. Where letters disagree with each other about a figure, both stand as written.
